Method

Popped to order, not to a shelf date

Most popcorn you buy was popped weeks ago and sealed in nitrogen. Ours is popped in the morning and packed the same afternoon, so the coating is still setting when it goes in the box.

  1. Heat. Mushroom kernels in a copper kettle with clarified butter, at the temperature the batch needs rather than one setting for everything.
  2. Coat. Seasoning goes on while the corn is still hot, tossed by hand in small lots so it grips instead of sliding off into the bottom of the bag.
  3. Rest. Twenty minutes on a cooling rack to let the glaze set hard — the step most kitchens skip, and the reason ours doesn't go soft in transit.
  4. Pack. Into a lined, resealable bag, labelled with the batch number and the date it came off the kettle.
